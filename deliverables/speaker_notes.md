# Slide 1

[About 45 seconds] This project asks whether economic narratives in news and social media help explain consumer sentiment that appears weak relative to headline economic indicators. I treat real household pressure, information framing and model limitations as competing explanations. The strongest finding is that the size of the sentiment gap changes substantially when the forecasting model changes. I completed a historical economic forecast test using genuine data vintages and a recent text-measurement pilot. The media evidence remains insufficient for either a causal claim or a validated claim of no effect. The research cutoff is October fourteenth, before the election. Sources: project assignment; research_report.pdf, pages 1 and 11.

# Slide 3

[About 55 seconds] Each forecast targets a calendar month and starts on its first day. Inputs come from the economic snapshot available by the previous day. I collected two hundred and two monthly ALFRED vintages from January twenty ten through October twenty twenty-six, and verified every requested vintage in the downloaded series headers. GDP remains quarterly. The latest released quarter supplies the growth input until the next release. The models train on an expanding window, using only sentiment outcomes already present in that origin vintage. Evaluation begins in twenty eighteen and covers one hundred and five final monthly outcomes. October preliminary sentiment is monitoring data and stays outside the main accuracy scores. The persistence benchmark follows ALFRED distribution availability, which can lag direct Michigan releases. Source: https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/help/downloaddata ; results/model_specifications.json.

# Slide 4

[About 60 seconds] This chart compares the same one hundred and five final months. Lower root mean squared error is better. Carrying forward the latest available sentiment reading has an error of four point seven five index points. The three-variable economic model has an error of fourteen point one zero. Adding recent sentiment lowers the economic model error to five point one nine, while household income and accumulated-price controls give five point five four. The simple macro relationship forecasts particularly poorly after the pandemic. This does not show that economic conditions or household pressures are irrelevant. It shows that the chosen linear specification does not extract a stable incremental signal beyond persistence. A six-month block bootstrap also shows a large loss disadvantage for the economic-only specification. Sources: results/forecast_metrics.json ; results/model_comparisons.json.

# Slide 5

[About 60 seconds] October preliminary sentiment is forty-six point three. The economic-only forecast is seventy-two point eight five, leaving a negative gap of twenty-six point five five points. With recent sentiment included, the forecast becomes fifty-three point one one and the gap shrinks to six point eight one points. Both observations lie inside the corresponding empirical prediction intervals. The economic-only interval is very wide, from forty-two point two six to one hundred and three point four three. That width signals limited predictive precision. A negative residual is unexplained under the selected model. It is not an estimate of irrationality, misinformation or a causal media contribution. The dashboard lets the reader change models and inspect this dependence directly. Sources: results/forecasts.csv, October 2026 rows.

# Slide 6

[About 60 seconds] The focused corpus has 316 economically relevant, US-related headlines and Reddit post titles, with 127 national news items, 33 local news items and 156 social posts. It is a convenience sample covering July through October, not a representative exposure measure. The classifier is a transparent Naive Bayes model trained on Financial PhraseBank. On four hundred and forty-three held-out company-finance sentences, macro F one is zero point seven nine six, compared with zero point five four three for a dictionary. Domain transfer is uncertain. The two methods disagree on 54.7 percent of the project items. External performance therefore cannot validate the media pilot. Independent human corpus labels remain missing. Sources: https://huggingface.co/datasets/takala/financial_phrasebank ; results/nlp_benchmark.json ; results/corpus_summary.json.

# Slide 7

[About 60 seconds] A separate retrospective test uses the San Francisco Fed historical news sentiment index, with the mean from two calendar months earlier. It changes macro-model RMSE from fourteen point one zero to thirteen point nine two, but changes the stronger dynamic model from five point one nine to five point two three. Full-window block-bootstrap intervals do not establish an improvement. A twenty twenty-one onward sensitivity shows a modest dynamic-model gain, so the result depends on the evaluation window. Historical index vintages were unavailable. These are reconstructed retrospective results, not a verified real-time signal. The own-text pilot remains too short for its own historical comparison and still lacks independent human validation. A causal framing experiment would need randomization, consent and appropriate review. Sources: https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/daily-news-sentiment-index/ ; results/news_index_comparison.json ; research_report.pdf pages 4 and 9.
